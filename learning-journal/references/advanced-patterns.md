# Learning Journal — Advanced Patterns

These patterns extend the core skill for serious learners managing knowledge across multiple domains.

---

## 1. Feynman Technique Integration

Use the "teach it to explain it" method as a retention tool:

```markdown
## Feynman Explanation: [Concept]
**Source:** [Capture reference]
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

### Explain It Simply (No Jargon)
[Write a 3-5 sentence explanation as if teaching a smart 12-year-old]

### Where I Got Stuck
[Parts where you couldn't simplify — these are your knowledge gaps]

### Refined Explanation (After Filling Gaps)
[Updated explanation after researching the gaps]

### Analogy
[Best analogy you can think of for this concept]
```

When you can't simplify it, you don't understand it. Agent flags concepts where the Feynman explanation has gaps and queues them for deeper study.

## 2. Knowledge Graph Building

Map connections between concepts across captures:

```markdown
## Knowledge Graph: [Domain]

### Nodes (Key Concepts)
1. [Concept A] — from [Source 1]
2. [Concept B] — from [Source 2]
3. [Concept C] — from [Source 3]

### Edges (Connections)
- A → B: [A enables/causes/contradicts B because...]
- B → C: [B builds on C by...]
- A → C: [A and C are both instances of...]

### Clusters
- **Cluster 1: [Theme]** — Concepts A, D, F
  - Shared insight: [what unifies them]
- **Cluster 2: [Theme]** — Concepts B, C, E
  - Shared insight: [what unifies them]

### Bridges (Cross-Cluster Connections)
- [Concept that connects Cluster 1 to Cluster 2]
- Why it matters: [insight from the connection]
```

Over time, this builds a personal knowledge map that reveals patterns across everything you've learned.

## 3. Book Club / Group Learning Mode

When learning with others (book clubs, study groups, mastermind):

```markdown
## Group Learning: [Book/Course Title]
**Group:** [Who's participating]
**Schedule:** [Cadence — weekly chapters, etc.]

### Session Log
| Date | Section | My Pre-Read Notes | Group Discussion Notes | Changed My Thinking? |
|------|---------|-------------------|----------------------|---------------------|
| 3/1 | Ch 1-3 | [My key takeaways] | [What others highlighted] | Yes — [how] |

### Perspective Tracker
| Concept | My Take | [Person B]'s Take | Synthesis |
|---------|---------|-------------------|-----------|
| [Topic] | [View] | [Different view] | [Combined insight] |
```

Other people's perspectives often reveal blind spots in your understanding.

## 4. Learning Sprint Protocol

Intensive learning periods with clear goals:

```markdown
## Learning Sprint: [Topic]
**Duration:** [2 weeks / 1 month]
**Goal:** [What you'll be able to do/understand/explain by the end]
**Daily commitment:** [30 min / 1 hour / 2 hours]

### Curriculum
| Day | Material | Time | Status | Notes |
|-----|----------|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | [Article/chapter] | 30m | ✅ | [Quick note] |
| 2 | [Video/course module] | 45m | ✅ | [Quick note] |
| 3 | Practice/apply | 1h | ⬜ | — |

### Milestone Checks
- [ ] Day 3: Can explain the basics to someone
- [ ] Day 7: Can apply to a real problem
- [ ] Day 14: Can teach it confidently

### Sprint Retrospective
- Completed: [X of Y items]
- Goal achieved? [Yes/Partially/No]
- Biggest insight: [...]
- What to learn next: [...]
- Retention plan: [Add to spaced repetition / Revisit in 30 days]
```

## 5. Capture-to-Content Pipeline

Turn your learning into content (teach to learn, learn to teach):

```markdown
## Content Pipeline: Learning → Publishing

### Stage 1: Capture (Automatic)
Every learning capture gets tagged with content potential:
- ⭐ High: Novel insight, contrarian take, surprising connection
- 🔵 Medium: Useful summary, practical application
- ⚪ Low: Personal reference only

### Stage 2: Develop (Weekly Review)
Pick 1 ⭐ capture per week and expand:
- What did I learn? (raw capture)
- Why does it matter? (so what?)
- How does it apply? (practical angle)
- What's the hook? (why would others care?)

### Stage 3: Draft
| Capture | Content Format | Platform | Status |
|---------|---------------|----------|--------|
| [Insight from Book X] | Thread | Twitter | Drafted |
| [Framework from Course Y] | Article | Newsletter | Outlined |

### Metrics
| Month | Captures | Content Published | Engagement | Best Performer |
|-------|----------|------------------|-----------|----------------|
| March | 15 | 4 | 230 avg | [Title] from [Source] |
```

The "teach what you learn" pipeline both improves retention AND builds your content library.

## 6. Anti-Library Tracking

Track what you intentionally decided NOT to learn and why:

```markdown
## Anti-Library (Decided Against)

| Item | Type | Why Considered | Why Rejected | Revisit? |
|------|------|---------------|-------------|----------|
| [Book X] | Book | Recommended by [person] | Too basic; already know this | No |
| [Course Y] | Course | Trending on Twitter | Doesn't align with Q1 goals | Maybe Q2 |
| [Topic Z] | Self-directed | Curiosity | Low ROI for current work | If role changes |
```

Knowing what NOT to learn is as valuable as knowing what to learn. Prevents "shiny object" learning syndrome.

## 10. Cross-Domain Insight Mining

The most valuable insights come from connecting ideas across different fields:

```markdown
## Cross-Domain Connections

### Connection: [Domain A] × [Domain B]
**From Domain A:** [Concept/principle]
**From Domain B:** [Concept/principle]
**The Connection:** [How they relate — analogy, shared principle, contradiction]
**Novel Insight:** [What you see by combining them that isn't obvious from either alone]
**Application:** [How to use this combined insight]

### Example
**From Ecology:** Keystone species — remove one species and the ecosystem collapses
**From Business:** Key person dependency — one employee leaving crashes operations
**The Connection:** Both are fragile systems with single points of failure
**Novel Insight:** The fix is the same in both — build redundancy and distribution
**Application:** When designing any system, identify and mitigate keystone dependencies
```

Agent periodically scans captures across domains and suggests potential connections during weekly reviews.
